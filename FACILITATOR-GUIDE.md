# Facilitator guide

Open the tool on a projector. Every scenario can be shared by link, so participants can follow on their own laptops
(**Share** button, top right).

## 60-minute executive session (Executive mode)

| Min | What | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 | Frame | "An MPS answers one question: can we make what customers want, week by week, with the capacity we have?" Show the four steps under **Learn**. |
| 5-15 | Read the baseline | Baseline scenario. Heat map set to *Utilisation*, grouped by Region, then Country. Ask: where is it hot? What happens in W52 and W53? Click a cell to see the plants behind it. |
| 15-30 | Europe demand +20% | Let the room propose fixes. Try overtime, then an extra shift, then a sourcing shift. Read the KPI tiles after each move: service level, revenue at risk, inventory, action cost. |
| 30-40 | Turkey bumper line fire | Move TR-BMP volume to PL-BMP. Discuss: who pays for the recovery, how long is the lane, what does Poland do next? |
| 40-50 | Port congestion + Asia sourcing | Remove the sea delay, add it back. Compare pipeline inventory with shortage. Add safety stock until the gap closes: what does it cost? |
| 50-60 | Decisions | Which levers are cheap, fast, slow? What data would we need from each plant to run this for real? |

## 90-minute planner workshop (Planner mode)

1. **Read a grid (15 min).** Open MX-TGT. Walk the rows top to bottom: requirement, safety stock, required production,
   capacity, required load, planned production, utilisation, projected stock, shortage.
2. **Heat map by plant (15 min).** Set *Required load*, group by Plant. List every plant above 100% and the week.
3. **Break and fix (25 min).** In pairs: Americas tariff pull-forward. Fix it using only levers a plant manager controls,
   then only levers a supply chain director controls.
4. **Be the planner (20 min).** Type your own numbers into planned-production cells for FR-FTK across the year-end
   shutdown. Beat the engine's stock level without breaking capacity.
5. **Debrief (15 min).** What did the engine assume that real life does not (frozen weeks, changeovers, tooling, labour)?

## Tips

- *Reset* returns to baseline. Presets also clear manual edits.
- The coloured bars under the week labels show firm call-offs (dark) versus forecast (light).
- All numbers are invented, so the debate stays on the logic rather than on whose data is right.
